# Generated figures

Regenerated by `python scripts/make_figures.py`, which reads only
`results/clean/`. **Never edit these by hand** — they are build outputs.

These are *this repository's* figures. The paper's own figures are in
`../` and are a frozen record; the two are not the same artifact and
should not be compared pixel for pixel.

## Text alternatives

Every figure has one, written in the same place as the plot rather than
added afterwards. Each says what the figure *shows*, not what it is, and
names the cleaned-output file holding the numbers behind it.

### `precipitation_states.png`

Paired bars showing the share of run-years in each of the five weather states, for the committed input and for a fresh sample drawn from the reconstructed transition matrix. The pairs are indistinguishable at every state and both sites; the largest difference is under 0.002, well inside sampling error. This is the check that the reconstructed matrix is right, since the original's exact draws cannot be recovered.

### `profit_against_rainfall.png`

Mean profit under perfect information against mean precipitation, for the four climates at each site, with a vertical line at the 72 cm where the yield curve peaks. The relationship is NOT monotone: climate 3 averages about 55 cm and earns less than climate 2 at about 50 cm, because climate 3 is uniform across all five weather states and puts a fifth of its years past the yield peak, where extra water reduces yield and the farm cannot shed it. The two sites lie almost on top of each other, which is the evidence that they sample the same four climates.

### `profit_by_scenario.png`

Four horizontal 95% confidence intervals for mean profit, one per scenario, for each site. Perfect Information is highest at about $2.36M for Equally Probable and $1.98M for Dry Most Likely; Known Climate sits just below; Stochastic and Expected Value are lower and lie almost on top of each other. The intervals are far narrower than the gaps between the first three, and the gap between the last two is invisible at this scale - which is the point the value of the stochastic solution makes numerically.

### `value_of_information.png`

Paired bars comparing the paper's four value-of-information figures with the reproduced ones, on a symmetric log scale because the quantities span $0.34 to $108,725. The pairs are visually identical for EVKW, EVKC and EVPI at both sites. The only visible difference is VSS at Dry Most Likely, where the reproduced bar is slightly taller ($964.89 against $940.90); at Equally Probable VSS is under a dollar and both bars are negligible. The numbers are in results/clean/value_of_information.csv.

### `yearly_by_scenario.png`

A three-by-two grid of line charts: water applied, crop yield and alternative water, over 25 years, for each site and each of the four scenarios. Every series is close to flat across years, because each year's weather is an independent draw and the investment is fixed at the start. Perfect Information applies the most water and gets the highest yield; Expected Value and Stochastic track each other closely. The numbers are in results/clean/yearly.csv.
